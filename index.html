What We Do

Residential pool cleaning, handled end to end

Every visit follows the same checklist so your water stays swim-ready in El Paso heat, dust storms, and monsoon season alike. Pick the weekly plan or call us for a one-time reset.

Weekly Pool Cleaning

Skim the surface, brush the walls and steps, vacuum the floor, empty skimmer and pump baskets, and hose down the deck. Same tech, same day each week, so you always know when we're coming.

  • Surface skim & net
  • Wall, step & bench brushing
  • Basket emptying

Chemical Balancing & Water Testing

We test chlorine, pH, alkalinity, cyanuric acid and hardness on every stop, then dose precisely for our hard El Paso tap water. Chemicals are included in your weekly rate — no surprise line items.

  • Full panel test each visit
  • Chlorine, pH & alkalinity correction
  • Stabilizer & hardness management

Green Pool Recovery

Algae bloom after a vacation or a dead pump? We shock, filter, drain-and-clean if the water is past saving, then hand it back swim-ready — usually inside three to five days.

  • Shock & algaecide treatment
  • Repeat filtering & vacuum-to-waste
  • Drain & acid wash when needed

Filter & Equipment Service

Cartridge cleans, DE and sand filter backwashing, pump basket and impeller checks, plus timer and automation adjustments. We flag failing equipment early, before it turns your pool green.

  • Filter cleaning & backwash
  • Pump & motor inspection
  • Timer & valve adjustment

Tile & Waterline Cleaning

Calcium scale and that grey waterline ring are unavoidable with El Paso's mineral content. We scrub tile and grout back to clean on a routine cycle so buildup never gets ahead of you.

  • Waterline tile scrubbing
  • Calcium & scale removal
  • Skimmer throat detailing

Vacation & One-Time Cleans

Heading out of town, hosting a party, or listing the house? Book a single deep clean or short-term coverage without signing up for an ongoing route. Easy in, easy out.

  • One-time deep clean
  • Short-term vacation coverage
  • Pre-listing & pre-party resets
